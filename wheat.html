🌾 Wheat Crop Analysis

📌 Crop Overview

Wheat is one of the most important cereal crops in India. It requires cool climate and fertile loamy soil.

🌡 Climate Requirements

Ideal Temperature: 10°C – 25°C

🌱 Suitable Soil

Loamy and clay loam soils with good drainage.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Need
Germination Medium
Flowering High
Grain Filling Very High

🧪 Fertilizers

  • Nitrogen → 120 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus → 60 kg/hectare
  • Potassium → 40 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 45–55 quintals/hectare

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 per hectare

✅ Advantages

  • High Market Demand
  • Good Yield
  • Suitable for Indian Climate
