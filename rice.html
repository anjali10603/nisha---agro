🌾 Rice Crop Analysis

📌 Crop Overview

Rice is one of the most important food crops in India. It requires warm climate and high water supply.

🌡 Climate Requirements

Ideal Temperature: 20°C – 35°C

🌱 Suitable Soil

Clayey and loamy soils with good water retention.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Need
Seedling High
Vegetative Very High
Flowering High

🧪 Fertilizers

  • Nitrogen → 100 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus → 50 kg/hectare
  • Potassium → 50 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 50–65 quintals/hectare

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹70,000 – ₹1,50,000 per hectare

⚠ Common Diseases

  • Blast Disease
  • Brown Spot
  • Bacterial Blight

✅ Advantages

  • High Market Demand
  • Essential Food Crop
  • Good Export Value
  • Profitable Farming
