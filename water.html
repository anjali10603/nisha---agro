💧 Drip Irrigation System

📌 System Overview

Drip irrigation delivers water directly to plant roots, reducing water wastage and improving crop growth.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Sugarcane
  • Cotton
  • Vegetables
  • Fruit Crops

💧 Water Saving Efficiency

Water saving up to: 40% – 60%

💰 Installation Cost

₹35,000 – ₹70,000 per hectare

✅ Advantages

  • Less Water Consumption
  • Better Crop Yield
  • Reduced Weed Growth
  • Improved Fertilizer Efficiency
🌧 Sprinkler Irrigation System

📌 System Overview

Sprinkler irrigation sprays water like rainfall over crops using pipes and rotating sprinklers.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Wheat
  • Maize
  • Pulses

💧 Water Efficiency

Saves approximately: 30% Water

💰 Installation Cost

₹25,000 – ₹50,000 per hectare

✅ Advantages

  • Uniform Water Distribution
  • Suitable for Large Farms
  • Reduces Soil Erosion
🤖 Smart Sensor Irrigation

📌 System Overview

AI-based smart sensors automatically monitor soil moisture and control irrigation systems.

📡 Features

  • Automatic Water Control
  • Real-Time Soil Monitoring
  • Mobile App Alerts
  • AI-Based Recommendations

📊 Sensor Accuracy

Approximate Accuracy: 95%

💰 Estimated Cost

₹50,000 – ₹1,20,000 depending on farm size.

🌊 Rainwater Harvesting

📌 System Overview

Rainwater harvesting stores rainwater for irrigation during dry seasons.

💧 Water Storage Capacity

  • Small Farm: 10,000 Liters
  • Medium Farm: 50,000 Liters
  • Large Farm: 1,00,000+ Liters

✅ Advantages

  • Groundwater Recharge
  • Reduced Water Scarcity
  • Eco-Friendly System
