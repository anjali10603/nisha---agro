🌾 Sugarcane Crop Analysis

📌 Crop Overview

Sugarcane is one of the most profitable commercial crops in India. It is mainly used for sugar production, jaggery and ethanol industries.

🌡 Climate Requirements

Ideal Temperature: 20°C – 35°C

🌱 Suitable Soil

Deep loamy soil with high fertility and proper drainage.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Need
Planting High
Vegetative Growth Very High
Maturity Medium

🧪 Fertilizers

  • Nitrogen → 150 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus → 60 kg/hectare
  • Potassium → 60 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 700–1000 quintals/hectare

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹1,50,000 – ₹3,00,000 per hectare

⚠ Common Diseases

  • Red Rot Disease
  • Smut Disease
  • Wilt Disease

✅ Advantages

  • High Sugar Demand
  • Profitable Commercial Crop
  • Used in Multiple Industries
  • Good Market Stability
