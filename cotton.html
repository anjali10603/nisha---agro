🌾 Cotton Crop Analysis

📌 Crop Overview

Cotton is an important commercial crop widely grown in India. It is mainly used for textile production and requires warm climate.

🌡 Climate Requirements

Ideal Temperature: 21°C – 30°C

🌱 Suitable Soil

Black cotton soil with good drainage is best for cotton farming.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Need
Seed Germination Medium
Flowering High
Boll Formation Very High

🧪 Fertilizers

  • Nitrogen → 100 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus → 50 kg/hectare
  • Potassium → 50 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 20–30 quintals/hectare

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000 per hectare

⚠ Common Diseases

  • Boll Rot
  • Leaf Curl Virus
  • Root Rot

✅ Advantages

  • High Market Demand
  • Important Textile Crop
  • Good Export Value
  • Highly Profitable
