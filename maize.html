🌽 Maize Crop Analysis

📌 Crop Overview

Maize is an important cereal crop widely grown in India. It is used for food, animal feed and industrial products.

🌡 Climate Requirements

Ideal Temperature: 18°C – 27°C

🌱 Suitable Soil

Well-drained loamy soil rich in organic matter is best for maize cultivation.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Need
Seed Germination Medium
Vegetative Growth High
Grain Filling Very High

🧪 Fertilizers

  • Nitrogen → 120 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus → 60 kg/hectare
  • Potassium → 40 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 45–70 quintals/hectare

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹60,000 – ₹1,40,000 per hectare

⚠ Common Diseases

  • Leaf Blight
  • Downy Mildew
  • Root Rot

✅ Advantages

  • High Nutritional Value
  • Used in Multiple Industries
  • Good Market Demand
  • Profitable Crop
