🌱 Sandy Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Sandy soil contains large particles and has excellent drainage capacity. It heats quickly and is suitable for drought-resistant crops.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Groundnut
  • Watermelon
  • Potato
  • Carrot
  • Coconut

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Sandy soil drains water rapidly and requires frequent irrigation.

Property Performance
Water Retention Low
Drainage Excellent
Fertility Medium

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Sandy soil supports fast root growth but requires regular fertilizers and irrigation.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Low Moisture Retention
  • Nutrient Loss
  • Frequent Irrigation Needed

✅ Advantages

  • Excellent Drainage
  • Easy Root Penetration
  • Warms Quickly
  • Good for Root Crops
  • Easy to Cultivate
