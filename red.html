🌱 Red Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Red soil is rich in iron oxide which gives it a reddish color. It is commonly found in southern and eastern India.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Groundnut
  • Millets
  • Potato
  • Tobacco
  • Pulses

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Red soil has moderate water retention and requires proper irrigation management.

Property Performance
Water Retention Moderate
Drainage Good
Fertility Medium

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Red soil supports good productivity when organic fertilizers are properly used.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Low Nitrogen Content
  • Requires Fertilizers
  • Can Dry Quickly

✅ Advantages

  • Good Drainage
  • Suitable for Multiple Crops
  • Easy Cultivation
  • Rich in Iron Minerals
  • Supports Pulse Crops
