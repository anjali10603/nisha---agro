🌱 Soil Guide


Different crops require different soil types.



Soil Types


✔ Loamy Soil → Wheat

✔ Black Soil → Cotton

✔ Clay Soil → Rice

🌱 Loamy Soil

📌 Soil Description

Loamy soil is considered the best soil for agriculture because it contains sand, silt and clay in balanced proportion.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Wheat
  • Sugarcane
  • Pulses

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Medium to High

📊 Fertility Level

Very High

🌱 Clay Soil

📌 Soil Description

Clay soil contains very fine particles and holds high moisture.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Rice
  • Broccoli
  • Cabbage

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Very High

📊 Fertility Level

High

🌱 Sandy Soil

📌 Soil Description

Sandy soil has large particles and fast drainage system.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Groundnut
  • Potato
  • Watermelon

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Low

📊 Fertility Level

Medium

🌱 Black Soil

📌 Soil Description

Black soil is rich in minerals and suitable for cotton farming.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Cotton
  • Soybean
  • Sunflower

💧 Water Holding Capacity

High

📊 Fertility Level

Very High

🌱 Red Soil

📌 Soil Description

Red soil is rich in iron but poor in nitrogen and phosphorus.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Millets
  • Groundnut
  • Tobacco

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Medium

📊 Fertility Level

Moderate

🌱 Alluvial Soil

📌 Soil Description

Alluvial soil is highly fertile and found in river plains.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Rice
  • Wheat
  • Sugarcane

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Medium to High

📊 Fertility Level

Excellent

🌱 Mountain Soil

📌 Soil Description

Mountain soil is found in hilly areas and rich in organic matter.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Tea
  • Coffee
  • Spices

💧 Water Holding Capacity

Medium

📊 Fertility Level

Good



Soil Testing


Regular soil testing improves crop quality and fertilizer balance.

🧪 Soil pH Analysis

Soil pH determines nutrient availability for crops. Ideal pH range: 6.0 – 7.5

📊 pH Level Chart

🌱 Nutrient Testing

Soil nutrients are essential for crop productivity. Major nutrients: Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium.

  • Nitrogen → Leaf Growth
  • Phosphorus → Root Development
  • Potassium → Crop Strength
💧 Soil Moisture Testing

Soil moisture analysis helps maintain proper irrigation and prevents overwatering.

📊 Moisture Analysis

  • Low Moisture → Dry Soil
  • Medium Moisture → Ideal
  • High Moisture → Waterlogging Risk
🌾 Organic Matter Testing

Organic matter improves soil fertility, water retention and microbial activity.

📊 Organic Content

  • Low Organic Matter → Poor Soil
  • Medium → Good Productivity
  • High → Excellent Fertility
