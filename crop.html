🌾 Wheat Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Wheat grows best in cool climatic conditions. Ideal temperature range: 10°C – 25°C. Excessive humidity may increase fungal diseases.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Loamy and clay loam soils with good drainage and pH level between 6.0–7.5 are ideal.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Requirement
Germination Medium
Tillering High
Flowering Very High

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus (P): 60 kg/hectare
  • Potassium (K): 40 kg/hectare
  • Organic Compost Recommended

🐛 Disease Detection

Common diseases affecting wheat crops:

  • Leaf Rust
  • Powdery Mildew
  • Karnal Bunt
  • Loose Smut

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 45–55 quintals/hectare

Average Harvest Duration: 120–150 days

Estimated Market Price: ₹2200–₹2800/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 per hectare depending on irrigation and fertilizer quality.

✅ Expert Recommendation

Use certified HYV seeds and adopt drip irrigation methods for higher productivity. Regular soil testing is recommended.
