🌾 Wheat Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Wheat grows best in cool climatic conditions. Ideal temperature range: 10°C – 25°C.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Loamy and clay loam soils with good drainage and pH level between 6.0–7.5 are ideal.

💧 Irrigation Management

Stage Water Requirement
Germination Medium
Tillering High
Flowering Very High

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Nitrogen (N): 120 kg/hectare
  • Phosphorus (P): 60 kg/hectare
  • Potassium (K): 40 kg/hectare

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 45–55 quintals/hectare

Estimated Market Price: ₹2200–₹2800/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹60,000 – ₹1,20,000 per hectare

🌾 Rice Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Rice requires warm and humid climate. Ideal temperature: 20°C – 35°C.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Clayey and loamy soil with high water retention.

💧 Irrigation Management

Rice cultivation requires continuous standing water.

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Nitrogen Rich Fertilizers
  • Phosphorus
  • Potash

🐛 Common Diseases

  • Blast Disease
  • Brown Spot

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 50–65 quintals/hectare

Estimated Market Price: ₹1800–₹2600/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹70,000 – ₹1,40,000

🌾 Cotton Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Cotton requires sunny weather. Ideal temperature: 21°C – 30°C.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Black cotton soil is most suitable.

💧 Irrigation Management

Moderate irrigation required during flowering stage.

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • NPK Fertilizers
  • Organic Compost

🐛 Common Diseases

  • Leaf Curl Virus
  • Wilt Disease

📊 Production Statistics

Estimated Market Price: ₹6000–₹8000/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹80,000 – ₹2,00,000

🌾 Sugarcane Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Sugarcane grows best in tropical climate. Ideal temperature: 20°C – 38°C.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Deep rich loamy soil with high fertility.

💧 Irrigation Management

Requires heavy irrigation and proper drainage.

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Organic Compost
  • Urea
  • Phosphate Fertilizers

🐛 Common Diseases

  • Red Rot
  • Smut Disease

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 600–800 quintals/hectare

Estimated Market Price: ₹300–₹400/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹1,00,000 – ₹2,50,000

🌾 Maize Crop Analysis

🌡 Climate Requirements

Maize grows best in warm climate. Ideal temperature: 18°C – 27°C.

🌱 Recommended Soil

Well-drained loamy soil is ideal.

💧 Irrigation Management

Requires medium irrigation during growth stage.

🧪 Fertilizer Schedule

  • Nitrogen Rich Fertilizers
  • Potassium
  • Organic Manure

🐛 Common Diseases

  • Leaf Blight
  • Downy Mildew

📊 Production Statistics

Average Yield: 35–50 quintals/hectare

Estimated Market Price: ₹1700–₹2400/quintal

💰 Profit Analysis

Estimated Net Profit: ₹50,000 – ₹90,000
