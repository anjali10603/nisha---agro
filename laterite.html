🌱 Laterite Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Laterite soil is formed in regions with high rainfall and temperature. It is rich in iron and aluminum but low in fertility.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Tea
  • Coffee
  • Cashew
  • Rubber
  • Coconut

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Laterite soil has moderate moisture retention and becomes hard when dry.

Property Performance
Water Retention Moderate
Drainage Good
Fertility Low to Medium

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Laterite soil becomes productive with proper fertilizers and irrigation systems.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Low Natural Fertility
  • Needs Organic Fertilizers
  • Can Become Hard in Summer

✅ Advantages

  • Good for Plantation Crops
  • Rich in Iron Minerals
  • Supports Tea & Coffee Farming
  • Good Drainage System
  • Suitable in High Rainfall Areas
