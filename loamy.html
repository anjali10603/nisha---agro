🌱 Loamy Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Loamy soil is considered the best soil for agriculture. It contains a balanced mixture of sand, silt and clay, making it highly fertile and ideal for most crops.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Wheat
  • Rice
  • Sugarcane
  • Vegetables
  • Maize

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Loamy soil has excellent moisture retention while still allowing proper drainage.

Property Performance
Water Retention Excellent
Drainage Very Good
Fertility High

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Loamy soil increases crop productivity up to 90% compared to poor-quality soils.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Can lose nutrients after heavy rainfall
  • Needs regular organic compost
  • Over-irrigation may affect drainage

✅ Advantages

  • Highly Fertile
  • Best for Multiple Crops
  • Good Water Balance
  • Improves Crop Yield
  • Easy to Cultivate
