🌱 Black Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Black soil is highly fertile and rich in minerals. It is famous for cotton cultivation and has excellent moisture retention.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Cotton
  • Soybean
  • Wheat
  • Groundnut
  • Sugarcane

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Black soil retains water for a long period, making it highly suitable for dry farming.

Property Performance
Water Retention Excellent
Drainage Moderate
Fertility Very High

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Black soil supports excellent productivity for cotton and commercial crops.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Becomes Sticky During Rain
  • Hard Cracks in Summer
  • Slow Drainage in Heavy Rain

✅ Advantages

  • Rich in Minerals
  • Excellent Moisture Storage
  • Best for Cotton Farming
  • High Crop Yield
  • Suitable for Commercial Crops
