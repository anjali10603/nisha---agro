🌱 Clay Soil Analysis

📌 Soil Overview

Clay soil contains very fine particles and has high water holding capacity. It is highly suitable for crops requiring more moisture.

🌾 Suitable Crops

  • Rice
  • Broccoli
  • Cabbage
  • Beans
  • Wheat

💧 Water Retention Capacity

Clay soil stores large amounts of water but may suffer from poor drainage.

Property Performance
Water Retention Very High
Drainage Low
Fertility High

🧪 Nutrient Levels

📈 Productivity Analysis

Clay soil supports high crop productivity for water-loving crops like rice.

⚠ Common Issues

  • Poor Drainage
  • Hard When Dry
  • Waterlogging Risk

✅ Advantages

  • High Fertility
  • Excellent Moisture Storage
  • Good for Rice Farming
  • Rich in Nutrients
  • Supports Long Crop Growth
